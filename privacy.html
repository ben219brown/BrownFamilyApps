Privacy Policy

Effective:

We design our apps to collect no personal information. We do not use third‑party analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not sell or share data with anyone.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, ad identifiers, or tracking pixels.
  • No fingerprinting or cross‑app/device tracking.
  • No crash reporters that send device/usage data to third parties.

What may be stored

Your content (like photos and notes) is stored locally on your device. If the app offers export/backup, it uses your chosen destination (e.g., your cloud drive) and never our servers.
