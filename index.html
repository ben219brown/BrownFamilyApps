How we build

Useful first. Transparent always.

01

Focused products

We would rather make a small app genuinely useful than bury the useful part under features nobody asked for.

02

Data minimization

We aim to collect only what is needed for app functionality, account services, purchases, or reliability — and explain it when we do.

03

No data-selling business model

We do not sell personal information or build advertising profiles from your app activity.

Privacy

Clearer than “we value your privacy.”

Different apps need different data to work. Our policy explains what each app uses, why it is used, which service providers are involved, and how to request deletion when an account exists.
