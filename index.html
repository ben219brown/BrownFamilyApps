What “no data collection” really means

Our apps do not include third‑party analytics, crash reporters, ads, or tracking pixels. We don’t build user profiles, and we do not sell or share any personal data. Where settings sync or backups are offered, they live on your device or in your own cloud account.

  • No ad SDKs or fingerprinting
  • No device‑level tracking or cross‑app IDs
  • All permissions are strictly opt‑in and only when needed
