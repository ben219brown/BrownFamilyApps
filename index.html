How we build

01

Focused products

We would rather make a small app genuinely useful than bury the useful part under features nobody asked for.

02

Data minimization

We aim to collect only what is needed for app functionality, account services, purchases, or reliability — and explain it when we do.

03

We do not sell personal information or build advertising profiles from your app activity.
