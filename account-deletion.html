Privacy controls

Account deletion

JobSnap users can request deletion of their account and associated Brown Family Apps cloud/server data.

How to request deletion

  1. Email jobsnapappinfo@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Account Deletion Request.
  3. Send the request from the email address associated with the JobSnap account when possible so we can verify ownership.
  4. After verification, we will process the request within 30 days unless limited retention is required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record obligations.
Start deletion request

What is deleted

We will delete the account credentials and Brown Family Apps cloud/server data associated with the verified JobSnap account, subject to any limited records we are required or permitted to retain.

What may remain elsewhere

Local files, exported files, Google Drive backups, app-store transaction records, or data held directly by third-party providers are controlled by the device owner or that provider and may need to be deleted separately.
