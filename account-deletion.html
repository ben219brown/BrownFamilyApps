Your data, your device

App data & account deletion

Most Brown Family Apps records are stored locally on your device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling generally removes that local copy. Sign-in information, game service data, external backups, store transactions and third-party service records may need separate deletion steps.

Local app data can be removed from your device. For apps without a separate account, clearing local storage is generally the main deletion step. If you use JobSnapApp sign-in, request account deletion separately as described below. Delete external backups from their storage destinations if you also want those copies removed.

JobSnapApp account deletion

JobSnapApp has its own published account deletion process for users of Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication. To request deletion of applicable sign-in/account information, email jobsnapappinfo@gmail.com with the subject Account Deletion Request. See the JobSnapApp privacy and account deletion policy for details. Deleting local app data does not automatically delete backups in your personal Google Drive account.

How to remove your app data

You can remove locally stored app data directly from your device. The exact menu names can vary slightly by device manufacturer and operating-system version.

Android

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Open Apps and select the Brown Family Apps app.
  3. Choose Storage or Storage & cache.
  4. Select Clear storage / Clear data.

You can also uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app and its local application data from the device.

iPhone / iPad

  1. Press and hold the app icon.
  2. Choose Remove App.
  3. Select Delete App.
  4. Confirm deletion.

Deleting the app removes its locally stored application data from the device.

What this removes

Clearing app storage or deleting the app removes information stored inside the app on that device, such as settings, locally maintained records, notes, and other app-specific data.

What may not be removed automatically

  • Files you intentionally exported, shared, downloaded, or saved somewhere outside the app.
  • Backups or copies stored in a cloud-storage account you control, plus any OS-managed device backups.
  • JobSnapApp sign-in data, Google Play Games data or app-store purchase and transaction records held by Google or another provider.
  • Diagnostic or crash-report information processed by a service provider, when an app uses such a service. Those records are governed by that provider's retention practices and are not part of a Brown Family Apps account.

Need help?

If you are unsure where an app stores its information or need help removing locally stored data, contact us and tell us which app and device you are using.
