Useful on day one

Less paperwork. Better records.

ServiceAll is built around the information you actually need when something is due, something breaks, or you are trying to remember what happened last time.

Photos + notes

Document what you saw, what you changed, and what the part looked like while the details are still fresh.

Flexible reminders

Use calendar dates or maintenance intervals based on mileage, hours, or recurring time periods.

Parts + components

Save part numbers, install dates, and linked service records for components you expect to replace again.

Cost history

Record service and repair costs so you can see what an asset is actually costing you over time.

Searchable history

Find an asset, past service, or recorded component without digging through receipts, notes, and camera rolls.

Portable service records

Service history is being designed with PDF/CSV export in mind, making records useful for resale, handoff, or your own archive.

Planned intelligence

Smarter help is on the roadmap.

The core app is about reliable maintenance records first. AI features are planned as focused tools that make those records faster and more useful—not as a replacement for qualified mechanical judgment.

Roadmap
PHASE 01

Photo → part identification

Photograph a filter, belt, label, or part tag and use recognition/OCR to help capture the brand or part number.

PHASE 02

Voice-to-record logging

Say what you changed and let ServiceAll turn the description into a structured service entry tied to the right asset.

PHASE 03

Troubleshooting assistance

Use symptoms plus asset history to surface likely causes, checks to perform, and parts that may deserve inspection.

Planned features may change during development. Troubleshooting assistance is intended as an aid, not a definitive diagnosis.
