Homesteader.
Keep the whole place straight.

Homesteader is being built as one practical home for the records that pile up around a homestead — animals, feed, ages, health checks, costs, gardening, notes, and the details you otherwise end up keeping in five different places.

Explore the planSend feedback

One record book for the homestead

Built around the things that actually need tracking.

Animal records

Keep ages, identifying details, notes, and important dates together for animals across the homestead.

Feed & supply costs

Track what you are spending on feed, supplies, and routine care so the numbers are easier to understand over time.

Health & vet checks

Record routine checks, treatments, appointments, and observations without relying on memory.

Garden tracking

Keep useful garden information, planting notes, and seasonal details close to the rest of your homestead records.

Costs & history

Build a useful history instead of losing receipts, notes, dates, and small expenses in separate notebooks.

Flexible by design

The goal is to support the variety of a real homestead rather than forcing every user into the same narrow workflow.

More than bookkeeping

Records on one side. Useful knowledge on the other.

Homesteader is also intended to keep practical animal and gardening reference information within reach, so the app is useful both when you are recording what happened and when you are deciding what to do next.
